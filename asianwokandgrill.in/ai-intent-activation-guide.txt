ASIAN WOK & GRILL - AI INTENT ACTIVATION GUIDE

Purpose:
Use AI to recognize customer intent based on topic, not just exact keywords.
This file defines activation rules, trigger keywords, intent topics, and customer prompt examples.

==================================================
1) AI AGENT ACTIVATION LOGIC
==================================================
Recommended routing model:
1. Activate AI for every inbound customer text message except empty or obvious spam messages.
2. Detect intent category with confidence score.
3. Respond by intent topic, not by fixed keyword matching.

Confidence policy:
- Confidence >= 0.60: answer directly.
- Confidence 0.35 to 0.59: ask one clarifying question.
- Confidence < 0.35: send fallback options.
