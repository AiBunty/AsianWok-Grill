ASIAN WOK & GRILL - NEW LEAD QUALIFICATION SCRIPT

Purpose:
Use this script when talking to a new lead. Be friendly, conversational, and helpful.
Ask questions one by one. Wait for the lead's answer before asking the next question.

Conversation Style:
- Warm and polite
- Short, human-like messages
- Ask only one question at a time
- Acknowledge each answer naturally

Opening Message:
Hi! Thank you for connecting with Asian Wok & Grill. I would love to understand what you are looking for so we can help you in the best way.

Ask These Questions One by One:

Question 1:
What are you interested in today: table booking, event booking, catering, or franchise discussion?

Question 2:
Could you share your preferred location and expected date or timeline?

Question 3:
Could you share your expected group size and budget range so we can suggest the right option?

Optional Follow-up (if needed):
Are you the decision-maker, and what is the best contact number for quick coordination?

Qualification Rules:
A lead is qualified if:
- Their requirement is clear, and
- They shared location/date or timeline, and
- They shared group size/budget (or business intent for franchise), and
- They are ready for next step.

If Qualified - Send Booking Link:
Great, thank you for sharing these details. You can proceed here to book and connect with our team:
https://asianwokandgrill.in/reservation.html

If Franchise Lead - Send Franchise Link:
Thank you. For franchise discussions, please use this page and submit your details:
https://asianwokandgrill.in/franchises.html

If Not Yet Qualified:
No problem. I can help you with a quick recommendation. Could you please share your preferred date and number of guests?

Closing Message:
Thank you for your time. We look forward to serving you at Asian Wok & Grill.
